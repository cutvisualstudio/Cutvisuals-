Pratham JainVideo Editor | Founder of CutVisuals

Pratham Jain, video editor.

I'm 19 and taught myself editing. Over 2+ years and 70+ projects, I've learned that a good edit is mostly decisions about what to cut and when.

I started on my own, learning by editing, breaking things and trying again. Today I cut YouTube videos, talking heads, documentaries, reels and motion graphics for creators and brands, working in Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve and Photoshop.

My approach is simple: understand the story first, then cut everything that doesn't serve it. I care about pacing, clean sound and the small details people feel without noticing, so every video holds attention from the first second to the last. If you have the footage and the idea, let's turn it into something people watch to the end.

FAQs

How do we get started?

Send me your footage and a short brief, or book a call. I'll suggest an approach and a timeline.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

That's fine. Show me what you have and what you want viewers to feel, and we'll work out the right edit together.

How many revisions do I get?

A reasonable number of revision rounds is included, so the final cut feels right.

How long does an edit take?

Short-form videos usually take 1–3 days. Longer videos take more, and I'll confirm after seeing your footage.

Can you match my style?

Yes. Share reference videos and I'll match the pacing, captions and look.
